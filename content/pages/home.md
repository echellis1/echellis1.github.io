I run end-to-end live event production and broadcast operations for a local
access cable television station in Exeter, NH — from live sports and school
board meeting coverage through scheduled cable playout — built on a
professional IP media toolchain of Dante audio and NDI video networking.

Alongside broadcast work, I have a long background in network systems
administration for K-12 education, and in live sound, lighting, and AV
systems integration for corporate events, concerts, and theater.

## What I Do

- **Live Production** — Multi-camera switching, graphics, and recording for
  live sports and school board meetings using vMix.
- **IP Media Infrastructure** — Dante audio networking and NDI video
  transport routing signals across the broadcast facility.
- **Audio Engineering** — Live sound mixing on Yamaha digital consoles,
  Q-SYS, and BSS audio processors across diverse venues.
- **Broadcast Ops** — Cable playout scheduling and on-air programming
  through TelVue automation.
- **Network Systems** — Enterprise wireless, VoIP, and server infrastructure
  administration, with 15+ years supporting school district IT environments.
